    About this data

    Thirteen multi-funder grant programs that include the Doris Duke Foundation (DDF), 2008–2026, grouped by DDF's program areas (Arts, Child Well-being, Environment, Medical Research). Program amounts are the total size of each program, not DDF's share, and come from the program's announcement (linked in each program's detail view). Endowments are FY23 assets under management or the latest total assets from tax filings. The dataset was compiled for the class reference visualization (ddf-constellation.vercel.app) and spot-checked against the linked sources. "Humanity AI" is placed under Arts because DDF has no separate AI program.
